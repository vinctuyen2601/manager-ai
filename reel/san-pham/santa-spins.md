## 5. Khối mô tả chuẩn — dán NGUYÊN VĂN vào mọi prompt có sản phẩm

Viết một lần, dùng lại mãi, để mọi cảnh mô tả **cùng một** sản phẩm. Sửa ở đây, không sửa trong từng prompt.

```
PRODUCT (keep exactly): Santa Spins, a fridge-magnet music player. A clear acrylic frame,
close to square, about the thickness of a desktop photo frame, with four round silver
screws in the four corners. Behind the acrylic: a white printed panel with a 3×3 grid of
Christmas album covers, a small line of black text across the top, and at the bottom a
printed progress bar and three printed play controls. In the centre: a round black
platter surrounded by a ring of small white LED beads. The frame has a white border. The LED ring is ALWAYS visible: when
off it is a frosted white ring of small dots; when on it glows warm white. A round music
disc with a black rim and a printed round label sits inside the LED ring. Proportions (from real footage): the
whole player is small, about one and a half hands wide (roughly 15 cm); the LED ring is
about half the player's width; a disc is a little smaller than the ring, about two fingers
wide when held between thumb and fingers. When OFF the ring is a thin grey ring around a
black platter; when a disc is placed it instantly glows warm white. There are nine discs, each with a DIFFERENT colorful album-style label,
no two alike. NOT round, NOT a vinyl record, NOT a speaker, NO screen.
```

⬜ Thêm **kích thước thật** vào khối này khi có (ví dụ "about 20 × 20 cm, a disc the size of a coaster") — AI hay vẽ sai tỉ lệ.

- Không nói có Bluetooth, có app, phát nhạc từ điện thoại (trang ghi "No app, no pairing").
